## Proposed 5 Attributes

Derived by looking at what all 4 characters need to differentiate from each other.  
Attribute scores range from **1–5** (matching d6 theme). Values used as modifiers to d6 rolls.

| # | German Name | Abbrev. | What it governs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Kraft** | KRA | Melee attack power, damage, breaking things |
| 2 | **Geschick** | GES | Speed, dodging, ranged attacks, number of actions |
| 3 | **Zähigkeit** | ZÄH | Max hit points, damage resistance, endurance |
| 4 | **Verstand** | VER | Magic power, trap detection, identifying items |
| 5 | **List** | LIS | Stealth, deception, pickpocketing, ambush |

## Character Profiles

### 1. Nordland Berserker

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Name** | *(Player chosen)* |
| **Herkunft** (Origin) | Nördliches Ödland |
| **Rasse** (Race) | Mensch |
| **Rolle** | Tank / Damage dealer |
| **Waffe** | Two-handed axe or war hammer |

**Concept:**  
A massive warrior from the frozen north. Slow but devastating. Becomes more dangerous as he takes damage — his rage grows. Low cunning, low magic, pure brute force and resilience. The "walk into the room and hit things" character.

**Special Mechanic — Berserkerrausch (Berserker Rage):**  
When reduced to half HP or below, gains a bonus to KRA or gets an extra attack. Exact rule TBD.

**Abilities (sketch):**
- **Plünderer** — Searches for loot faster, can break locked doors/chests
- **Unaufhaltsam** — Ignores the first status effect each scenario (stunned, slowed, etc.)
- **Berserkerrausch** — Rage mechanic when wounded (see above)

**Starting Equipment (ideas):**  
Heavy armour, two-handed weapon, healing rations (2×)

### 2. Elfische Kriegstänzerin

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Name** | *(Player chosen)* |
| **Herkunft** (Origin) | Elfenreich |
| **Rasse** (Race) | Elf |
| **Rolle** | Skirmisher / Multi-attacker |
| **Waffe** | Paired short swords or sword + dagger |

**Lore:**  
Kriegstänzerinnen are always female, trained from childhood in the sacred arts of combat to protect the **Heiliger Hain**. Upon completing their training they embark on a journey lasting one full human lifespan — travelling the world, learning the ways of other races, before returning home with hard-won wisdom. The Kriegstänzerin in the party is mid-journey. She has seen much. She judges what she sees — and goblins, in particular, fail every test.

**Special Mechanic — Kriegstanz (War Dance):**  
Makes **2 attacks per action** instead of 1. Each attack rolls separately. Lower damage per hit, but two chances to connect and apply effects.

**Abilities (sketch):**
- **Kriegstanz** — 2 attacks per action (lower damage per hit vs. Berserker)
- **Elfensinne** — Can detect hidden doors, traps, and ambushes automatically
- **Schattenschritt** — Can move through enemy spaces (doesn't trigger opportunity effects)

**Inter-character tension:**  
Has low tolerance for the Goblin Halsabschneider (cultural animosity — elves find goblins repulsive). May cause friction or provide comedy. *(Potential mechanic: disadvantage on cooperation rolls between these two.)*

**Starting Equipment (ideas):**  
Light armour, two short swords, 1 healing potion

### 3. Goblin Halsabschneider

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Name** | *(Player chosen — or a guild alias)* |
| **Herkunft** (Origin) | Menschenreich (Stadtunterwelt / City underworld) |
| **Rasse** (Race) | Goblin |
| **Rolle** | Assassin / Rogue / Scout |
| **Waffe** | Short blade + throwing knives |

**Lore:**  
One of the few goblins who moves freely across all three realms — a rare and dangerous thing. May still answer to the guild network, or may have gone fully freelance. Nobody is quite sure. Knows every fence, smuggler, and corrupt city guard between here and the Ödland. Has a complicated working relationship with the Kriegstänzerin (she despises him; he finds this funny).

**Special Mechanic — Meucheln (Assassination Strike):**  
If attacking a target that hasn't activated this round (surprised or from stealth), deals **bonus damage** — possibly double. Exact mechanic TBD.

**Special Mechanic — Goblinzauber (Drunk Magic):**  
Goblins can only access magic when **extremely drunk**. After consuming strong alcohol (a consumable item), the Halsabschneider may cast one random spell — drawn from a small deck or table. The effect is unpredictable but real. Costs a consumable slot and sobriety.

**Abilities (sketch):**
- **Durchwuseln (Passiv)** — Can move freely through spaces occupied by allies and small minions without stopping or penalty.
- **Meucheln** — Bonus/double damage when striking first or from stealth
- **Schlösserknacker** — Automatically opens locks or greatly improves the roll
- **Im Schatten verschwinden** — Once per scenario, can become hidden even in the open
- **Goblinzauber** — Cast a random spell while drunk (see above)

**Inter-character tension:**  
The Kriegstänzerin finds him repulsive. He finds this hilarious. The Magiegelehrte considers his drunk spellcasting a personal insult to the Kaiserliche Akademie.

**Starting Equipment (ideas):**  
No armour (light leather), short blade, 3 throwing knives, lockpicks, 1 flask of goblin spirits

### 4. Magiegelehrte der Kaiserlichen Akademie für Arkane Künste

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Klassenbezeichnung** | Magiegelehrte der Kaiserlichen Akademie für Arkane Künste |
| **Name** | *(Player chosen)* |
| **Herkunft** (Origin) | Menschenreich |
| **Rasse** (Race) | Mensch |
| **Rolle** | Ranged Damage / Crowd Control / Support |
| **Waffe** | Staff + spells |

**Lore:**  
A graduate of the most prestigious (and expensive) magical institution in the realm. Trained in structured, classified, academically rigorous arcane arts — the polar opposite of whatever the goblin does with cheap spirits. She knows she is the most educated person in the room and occasionally forgets that educated doesn't mean invincible. She is on a field assignment — whether voluntary or required by the Akademie is up to the player.

**Special Mechanic — Zaubern (Spellcasting):**  
Uses **VER** for spell attacks. Has a limited spell resource per scenario — exact system TBD (spell slots, fatigue track, or dice pool).

**Abilities (sketch):**
- **Feuerstoß** — Ranged fire attack, can ignite enemies (Brennend condition)
- **Eisige Ketten** — Freezes/slows an enemy for 1 turn (crowd control)
- **Magischer Schild** — Temporary defence buff to self or an ally

**Inter-character tension:**  
Views the Goblin's drunk magic as an insult to the Akademie and to magic as a discipline. Has a wary mutual respect with the Kriegstänzerin — two professionals who acknowledge each other's competence.

**Starting Equipment (ideas):**  
Staff, no armour (robes), 2 spell slots per scenario, 1 healing potion

**Open Questions for Magiegelehrte:**

- [ ] Can she learn new spells as she levels up?
- [ ] What spells does she start with?

## 🎭 Optional Character Traits (Vor- & Nachteile / Wesenszüge)

> **Design Note for Future Exploration:**  
> We may want to formalize **1 positive and 1 negative trait** per character to deepen gameplay identity, roleplay, and table banter:
> - **Goblin Halsabschneider:**
>   - *Positiv:* **Durchwuseln** (moves through allies & small minions freely) / **Schlösserknacker**.
>   - *Negativ:* **Goldgier / Kleptomanie** (must pick up gold immediately, or disadvantage when resisting bribery) OR **Dunkelmagie-Sucht** (can only cast spells when hammered).
> - **Elfische Kriegstänzerin:**
>   - *Positiv:* **Elfensinne / Kriegstanz** (detects hidden doors/traps; 2 attacks per action).
>   - *Negativ:* **Kulturelle Arroganz / Abscheu** (disadvantage on cooperative actions with the Goblin; refuses to use crude/blunt weapons).
> - **Nordland Berserker:**
>   - *Positiv:* **Berserkerrausch / Unaufhaltsam** (deals bonus damage when wounded; ignores stun once per dungeon).
>   - *Negativ:* **Blutrausch / Ehrgeiz** (cannot retreat or move away while an adjacent enemy is alive; cannot flee combat).
> - **Magiegelehrte der Akademie:**
>   - *Positiv:* **Akademische Analyse** (can identify magic relics, decipher ancient runes, detect arcane traps automatically).
>   - *Negativ:* **Glaskinn / Arroganz** (physically fragile; refuses to carry heavy armor or crude shields; penalties when forced into mundane physical labor).

## Shared Mechanics (applies to all characters)

- **Lebenspunkte (LP)** — Hit Points, derived from ZÄH
- **Aktionen pro Runde** — Actions per turn (1 standard; Kriegstänzerin gets 2 attacks)
- **Initiative** — Determined by GES (highest goes first)
- **Levelaufstieg** — Character progression (to be designed in Phase 3)
