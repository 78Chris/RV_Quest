# RV Quest — 8-Week Christmas Gift Schedule

> **Goal:** A complete, printable, laser-cut-ready compact dungeon crawl game by ~December 25.  
> **Time budget:** ~1 hour/day on weekdays = ~5 hours/week = **~40 hours total**  
> **Starting:** Week of October 27, 2026

---

## 🎯 Deliverables by Christmas

By end of Week 8 you should have:
- ✅ Complete rulebook (printable, ~8-12 pages)
- ✅ 2–4 playable characters with printed sheets and laser-cut standees
- ✅ 8–12 monsters with printed stat cards
- ✅ Modular dungeon tiles (laser cut)
- ✅ All tokens, doors, treasure chests (laser cut)
- ✅ 3 playable scenarios (including a tutorial)
- ✅ A nice printed box or storage solution

## ⚠️ Risk Factors

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Rules take longer than expected | Keep it lean — if in doubt, cut a feature |
| Playtest reveals big problems | Week 6 is fully reserved for this |
| Print/cut takes longer than 1 day | Start cutting in Week 7 if possible |
| Laser cutter issues | Have a card+cardboard fallback for tiles |

---

## 💡 Tips for Success

- **Keep scope tight.** 2 heroes, 8 monsters, 3 scenarios is a complete game.
- **Rules first, aesthetics later.** Don't spend time on beautiful layouts until Week 7.
- **Write decisions down immediately** in `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` so sessions resume fast.
- **Playtest early** — even a paper prototype in Week 3 is better than waiting.
