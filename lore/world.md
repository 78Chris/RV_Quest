## The Races

### Humans — Kaisertum Valdor
- Dominant in the central and eastern plains
- Imperial seat, feudal baronies, bustling walled cities, and trading guilds
- Seat of the **Kaiserliche Akademie für Arkane Künste**
- Pragmatic, bureaucratic, militaristic, and ambitious

### Elves — Sylvanheim
- Realm in the deep, primeval western forests
- Ancient, proud, deeply connected to nature and arcane currents
- Not pacifists: home of the elite **Elfische Kriegstänzerinnen**, defenders of the spiritual heart — the **Heiliger Hain**
- Relationship with humans: haughty condescension mixed with diplomatic caution

### Goblins — Die Schattenlose Gesellschaft & Der Schattenbazar
- **No dedicated realm or sovereign borders** — goblins are ubiquitous and embedded across all realms
- Rather than a single mafia, it is simply the natural goblin way of living and surviving among larger folk
- They operate **Der Schattenbazar** — a sprawling, decentralized medieval "darknet" trading network for illegal goods, poisons, smuggled curiosities, stolen relics, and contract work
- Local goblin guilds act as informal spheres of influence, turf bosses, and fences
- Tolerated by human barons and elven wardens because they move goods that official channels cannot

### Nördliches Ödland (Northern Wildlands)
- Harsh, wind-swept, Scandinavian-inspired northern wastes
- Not a unified kingdom: a patchwork of rugged clans, ancestor shrines, and fortified longhouses
- Culture: warrior ethos, harsh survival, raiding, personal honor
- Home of the **Nordland Berserker** (hailing from the Nördliches Ödland)
- Feared by the imperial citizens of Valdor as untamed and savage

## World-Specific Rules & Flavour

- **Der Schattenbazar (The Shadow Bazaar):** In any settlement or outpost, a goblin contact can be found. They offer rare poisons, lockpicks, smuggled trinkets, and whispers of forgotten dungeons.
- **Magic Policed vs. Wild:** The **Kaiserliche Akademie für Arkane Künste** in Valdor attempts to regulate all magic through strict dogma, parchment licenses, and arcane theory.
- **Goblin Magic (Goblinzauber):** Goblins scoff at arcane theory; their connection to the raw ether is unlocked only through profound intoxication. To an Akademie scholar, this is an obscene affront; to the goblin, it gets results.

## Racial Lore

### Elfische Kriegstänzerinnen
- **Always female** — trained from childhood as elite warriors
- Their sole purpose: protect the **Heiliger Hain** (Sacred Grove — the spiritual heart of the Elfenreich)
- Upon completing their training, they travel the world for **one entire human lifespan** (~80 years; a short time for an elf)
- Purpose of the journey: to scout other races, gather knowledge, and return worldly wisdom to their people
- The Kriegstänzerin in the party is mid-journey — she has seen much, and judges what she sees

### Nordland Berserker (Nordländer)
- Nordländer rarely travel south — their world is the Ödland, and it is enough
- Only the **very best warriors** leave, and only after defeating every worthy opponent at home
- They go south to seek **glory and worthy battles** — not treasure, not politics
- The Berserker in the party is such a warrior: undefeated, seeking greater challenges
- Returning home with great deeds earns legendary status among the clans

### Goblin Halsabschneider
- Deeply embedded in the shadow economy — knows every fence, smuggler, and crooked guard
- One of the few goblins who operates freely across all three realms (a valuable and dangerous thing)
- Magic ability: unlocked only through extreme intoxication — not reliable, not respected, but real
- Tension with the Kriegstänzerin is almost certain to cause problems — and entertainment

### Magiegelehrte der Kaiserlichen Akademie für Arkane Künste
- The Kaiserliche Akademie is the most prestigious (and most expensive) magical institution
- Graduates are highly skilled and very aware of it — a reputation for arrogance is well-earned
- She has access to structured, trained magic — the opposite of goblin drunk-casting
- Her relationship with the Goblin is professional contempt; with the Kriegstänzerin, wary mutual respect
